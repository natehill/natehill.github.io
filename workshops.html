Workshops | Performance

Workshops are where my day job sometimes intersects with my creative practice. As the director of a nonprofit that often focuses on helping libraries, archives, and other cultural insitutions imagine positive futures for their organizations, I find this is where object or image-making intersects with a kind of social practice and community work.
